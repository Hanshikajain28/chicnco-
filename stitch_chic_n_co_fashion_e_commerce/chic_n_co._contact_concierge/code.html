Frequently Asked Questions

Explore immediate answers regarding our sizing philosophy, delivery timeframes, fabric care, and atelier return processes.

Each product detail page incorporates an interactive Fit Assistant alongside exact garment flat measurements (bust, waist, hip, and total drape length). If you find yourself between two numerical sizes:

  • For relaxed silhouettes and fluid silk blousons, we recommend selecting your true standard size.
  • For structured corset tops, bonded satin evening gowns, and tailored pencil skirts, we suggest sizing up or reaching out to live chat for a tailored measurement cross-reference.

We honor a complimentary 30-day return and exchange window from the date of shipment delivery. Garments must remain unworn, unwashed, and in pristine condition with all original security tags and garment bags intact.

Domestic exchanges for alternative sizes are entirely complimentary with prepaid priority labels generated instantly via our self-serve Returns Portal.

All orders placed before 2:00 PM EST are dispatched from our New York fulfillment facility the same business day.

  • Complimentary Standard Express: 2–4 business days within the continental US.
  • Next-Day Priority Air: 1 business day (available during checkout).
  • International Express Courier (DHL): 3–5 business days worldwide with prepaid duties and taxes handled at checkout.

Our pure mulberry silks and heavyweight recycled satins benefit most from gentle eco-friendly dry cleaning or lukewarm hand-washing with pH-neutral silk wash. Always steam on low heat from the reverse side of the garment, and avoid direct perfume contact on silk fibers to maintain pristine luster.
