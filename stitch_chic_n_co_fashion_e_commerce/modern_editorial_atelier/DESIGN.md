## Brand & Style

This design system embodies a modern, feminine high-fashion e-commerce experience that merges editorial sophistication with effortless digital commerce. The tone is poised, confident, aspirational, and inclusive—evoking the intimacy of a luxury atelier alongside the sleek precision of contemporary digital flagships.

The visual style synthesizes **Modern Editorial Minimalism** with **Tactile Soft-Luxury**:
- Generous breathing room and architectural negative space prioritize high-caliber apparel imagery over decorative noise.
- Editorial typographic pairings marry high-contrast display serifs with ultra-legible, geometric sans-serifs.
- Tactile warmth is woven through soft alabaster foundations, delicate blush nuances, whisper-weight hairline borders, and pill-shaped interactive elements.
- Micro-interactions are fluid, gentle, and deliberate, mimicking the drape and cadence of fine fabric.

## Colors

The palette balances clean architectural contrast with warm organic undertones:

- **Primary (`#18181B`)**: Deep charcoal/soft black serves as the primary anchor for dominant typography, primary navigation links, key CTA buttons, and high-impact structural lines. Avoids pure digital black (`#000000`) for a softer, editorial ink aesthetic.
- **Secondary (`#BE185D`)**: Deep rose/raspberry pink acts as a strategic focal point for promotional banners, sale indicators, active favorites, and curated editorial tags. Supported by blush tints (`#FDF2F8`, `#FCE7F3`) for subtle tinting, badges, and contextual wash surfaces.
- **Tertiary (`#D4AF37`)**: Subtle antique gold provides muted accentuation for loyalty tiering, luxury markers, jewelry callouts, and limited-edition badges.
- **Neutral Base (`#FDFBF7`)**: Warm alabaster canvas replaces harsh pure white as the core screen background, introducing organic tactility. Layered alongside crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) for elevated product tiles and cards, `#F5F0EB` for secondary surface panels, and `#E8E0D5` for muted hairline borders.
- **Text Hierarchies**: Deep charcoal (`#18181B`) for display titles, intermediate zinc (`#52525B`) for body descriptions, and light zinc (`#A1A1AA`) for subtle metadata and inactive placeholders.

## Typography

The typographic system creates an interplay between timeless editorial refinement and sharp contemporary usability:

- **Display & Headline Hierarchy (`Playfair Display`)**: Employs classical transitional serifs with graceful stroke transitions. Reserved strictly for collection names, campaign headlines, marquee banners, and section titles. Never set in all-caps across multi-word sentences to preserve graceful vertical cadence.
- **Interface & Body Hierarchy (`Plus Jakarta Sans`)**: Delivers crisp geometric structure with rounded humanist terminals, offering optimal legibility for merchandise attributes, garment specifications, long-form reviews, and tabular checkout workflows.
- **Labels & Microcopy**: Upper-cased label roles (`label-md`, `label-sm`) require tracking expanded between `0.04em` and `0.08em` to convey an upscale boutique tone across navigation, breadcrumbs, tags, and garment sizing chips.
- **Numeric & Pricing Rules**: All product pricing is explicitly rendered in `Plus Jakarta Sans` rather than serif to convey instant transactional clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout structure adheres to an airy, editorial grid system designed to frame high-resolution photography without clutter:

- **Grid Architecture**: 
  - **Desktop (≥ 1280px)**: 12-column responsive grid with a `3rem` (48px) exterior margin, `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, and a maximum content constraint of `1440px`.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1279px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` (32px) margins and `1.25rem` (20px) gutters.
  - **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with `1.25rem` (20px) margins and `1rem` (16px) gutters, preserving edge breathing space for narrow viewports.
- **Product Display Rhythm**: 
  - Desktop product grids default to 4 columns or alternating asymmetrical 2-column/3-column editorial spreads.
  - Mobile product feeds transition to a 2-column cadence or high-fashion 1-column full-width storytelling stream.
- **Spacing Logic**: Spacing scales follow a deliberate base-4 rhythm. Macro separation between distinct campaign chapters uses `space-xl` (and multiples thereof), while component-level density operates strictly within `space-xs` through `space-md`.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a featherweight tactile model featuring warm ambient diffusion, translucent frosted surfaces, and refined hairline frames rather than heavy dropped shadows:

- **Base Layering**: The primary foundation rests on alabaster (`#FDFBF7`). Interactive surfaces (e.g., product cards, dropdown sheets, modal overlays) rely on crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) to visually rise without relying entirely on dark shadows.
- **Ambient Shadow Craft**:
  - *Resting Card Elevation*: `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.03)`.
  - *Hover Elevation*: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.07), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.03)`. Tinted softly with warm charcoal undertones rather than neutral greys.
  - *Floating CTAs & Drawers*: `0 20px 40px -12px rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.12)`.
- **Translucency & Frosting**: Sticky navigation headers, announcement tickers, and floating filter bars leverage glassmorphism: `background: rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85); backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`.
- **Hairline Enclosures**: Subtle 1px borders using `#E8E0D5` (with dark opacity falls down to `rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.06)`) frame interactive zones cleanly, maintaining a bespoke, tailored aesthetic.

## Shapes

The design system applies a balanced curvature philosophy (`roundedness: 2`), harmonizing soft organic geometry with structural elegance:

- **Cards & Visual Containers**: Product tiles, editorial spotlights, lookbook inserts, and slide-in panels utilize `rounded-lg` (1rem / 16px) and `rounded-xl` (1.5rem / 24px) to soften the perimeter of tall fashion imagery.
- **Inputs & Form Controls**: Text boxes, search wrappers, and checkout entry fields apply `rounded-md` (0.5rem / 8px) to establish a clean, architectural utility.
- **CTAs, Badges & Pills**: Primary action buttons, category tags, notification badges, and size pickers use full capsule geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px), introducing a friendly, modern contrast against sharp typography.

## Components

### Buttons & CTAs
- **Primary**: Full pill shape (`rounded-full`), deep charcoal background (`#18181B`), crisp white text, `label-lg` uppercase typography with `0.04em` tracking. Padding: `14px 28px`. Subtle elevation on hover with scale transition (`scale-[1.01]`).
- **Secondary / Outline**: Pill shape, 1px border (`#18181B`), transparent or pure white fill, charcoal label. Transitions to charcoal fill with white text upon hover.
- **Tertiary / Accent**: Soft blush fill (`#FCE7F3`), rose text (`#BE185D`), ideal for "Save to Wishlist", "Gift Registry", and seasonal promotions.
- **Floating Bag / Quick-Add**: Circular pill button pinned subtly over product imagery or floating at bottom on mobile viewports.

### Product Cards
- **Structure**: Clean 3:4 portrait aspect ratio for imagery. Base container uses `rounded-lg` with pure white background (`#FFFFFF`) and a subtle 1px border (`#E8E0D5`).
- **Image Behavior**: Smooth crossfade to secondary lookbook view on desktop hover.
- **Badges**: Pill-shaped overlays placed top-left (e.g., "NEW SEASON", "ORGANIC SILK", "LIMITED") with `#FDF2F8` fill, `#BE185D` text, uppercase `label-sm`.
- **Wishlist Action**: Top-right floating circular button (`36px × 36px`) with translucent white fill (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`), hairline border, housing a minimal stroke heart icon.
- **Metadata**: Left-aligned beneath the frame. Title in `Playfair Display` (`headline-sm` / `title-editorial`), color variant swatches displayed as minimal circles, and price anchored in `Plus Jakarta Sans` bold.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category & Filter Chips**: Capsule pills (`rounded-full`) with `10px 18px` padding. Resting state: alabaster/white surface with `#E8E0D5` border and charcoal text. Active state: deep charcoal background (`#18181B`) with crisp white text.
- **Size Selector Pills**: Compact squircle or pill shapes (`40px × 40px` or `44px × 32px`). Out-of-stock states feature muted light zinc borders (`#E4E4E7`) with a diagonal hairline cross-through.

### Inputs & Form Elements
- **Text Inputs**: Height of `48px`, `rounded-md`, filled with pure white (`#FFFFFF`), outlined with a 1px border in `#E8E0D5`. Focus state activates a 1.5px deep charcoal border (`#18181B`) with a soft blush ambient glow (`rgba(244, 114, 182, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios**: Checkboxes use `rounded-sm` (4px); radio elements are circular. Selected state features `#18181B` fill with a crisp white checkmark or center dot.

### Announcement Ticker & Header Navigation
- **Sticky Announcement Ticker**: Top ribbon with warm blush background (`#FCE7F3`), deep charcoal `label-sm` copy, accompanied by a subtle horizontal marquee animation highlighting seasonal offers, complimentary shipping, and atelier drops.
- **Navigation Bar**: Semi-transparent alabaster frost (`rgba(253, 251, 247, 0.85)` with `backdrop-blur-md`), 1px bottom border (`rgba(24, 24, 27, 0.05)`). Center-weighted brand wordmark flanked by clean collection links in `label-md` tracking and streamlined micro-action icons (search, profile, wishlist counter, bag).

### Lookbook / Editorial Banners
- **Split Hero Units**: Asymmetric dual-column cards pairing full-bleed lifestyle photography with warm alabaster text panels featuring `display-hero` typography, drop-cap styling, and pill-shaped link CTAs.
