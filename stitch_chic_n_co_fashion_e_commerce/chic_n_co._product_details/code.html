A masterclass in effortless feminine drape. The Florentine Maxi is crafted from high-density mulberry silk blended with breathable Eco-Vero viscose, tailored to create liquid-like accordion micro-pleating that dances with every stride.

  • Adjustable French halter neckline with delicate gold-capped silk ties
  • Concealed side zipper with reinforced internal hook-and-eye closure
  • Fully lined in 100% breathable organic modal silk through mid-thigh
  • Ankle-grazing sweeping hemline with hand-rolled edge finishing

Shell: 70% Certified Eco-Vero™ Viscose, 30% Grade-6A Mulberry Silk.
Lining: 100% Breathable Organic Modal.
Certified Standard 100 by OEKO-TEX® (Zero toxic dye agents or microplastics).

Care Instructions:

Dry clean gently or delicate cold hand-wash with silk-safe detergent. Lay flat on dry cotton towel in shade. Steam on lowest silk heat setting; do not press iron directly onto pleats.
